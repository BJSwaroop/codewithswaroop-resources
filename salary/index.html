🤔 Why Negotiate At All?

Most freshers don't know this — a small difference in starting salary becomes a huge difference over your career.

The Compounding Effect
Every hike and every job switch is calculated as a percentage of your current CTC. Start ₹1 LPA higher, and every future offer is benchmarked against the bigger number.
Example: 4.5 LPA vs 5.5 LPA start. Across 3 job switches (avg 30% hike each), the 5-year difference = ₹8-12 lakhs+ total. From one email.
"Will They Withdraw My Offer?" — The Fear, Debunked
This is the biggest fear — and it's almost never true. The company already spent weeks on your hiring: interviews, HR rounds, approvals. After all that effort, nobody cancels an offer because you politely asked a question.
When IS an offer at risk? Being demanding, giving ultimatums, or faking competing offers. None of those are in these scripts.
Golden Rule: Your leverage is highest after the offer letter arrives and before you accept. That's your window. Negotiating after accepting looks unprofessional.
Swaroop's Take: Asking is free. Worst case, they say no — you accept gracefully and lose nothing. Best case, ₹1-3 LPA extra. Zero risk, lakhs of upside. Ask!
📧 The Email Template

This is the exact email — copy it, fill the yellow blanks, and send. Tone: grateful + confident + flexible.

Email Script
Subject: Regarding Compensation — [Role] Offer | [Your Name]

Dear [HR Name],

Thank you so much for offering me the [Role] position at [Company]. I'm genuinely excited about the opportunity and the team I'd be working with.

Before I formally accept, I wanted to ask if there is any flexibility in the compensation. Based on my skills in [Skill 1] and [Skill 2], and the current market range for this role, I was hoping for a package closer to ₹[X] LPA.

I want to be transparent — [Company] is my first choice, and if we can work this out, I'm ready to accept immediately.

Thank you for considering my request. Looking forward to hearing from you.

Warm regards,
[Your Name]
[Phone Number]
Why this works: It opens with gratitude (goodwill), gives a specific number with a reason (serious candidate), and ends with "ready to accept immediately" (an easy close for HR). Those 3 elements get positive replies.
⚠️ The [X] value: Ask for 10-20% above the offered package. For a 4.5 LPA offer, ask 5-5.4. Asking for double ends the conversation.
📞 The Call Script

When HR calls, don't panic — follow this flow. Exact words for every step.

1
Start With Thanks
"Thank you so much for the offer! I'm really excited about the role and the company."
Never open with the salary topic.
2
The Ask — Polite & Specific
"I had one small question — is there any flexibility in the compensation? Based on my skills and the market range for this role, I was hoping for something closer to ₹[X] LPA."
3
The Pause — Silence Is Power
After you ask, stay silent. Even if it feels awkward. Don't nervously fill the gap with "sorry, it's okay if not possible..." — that weakens your ask. Let HR respond first.
4
If They Say "Let Me Check"
"That would be great, thank you! Should I wait for your confirmation before signing the offer letter?" — fix a timeline and establish your right to follow up.
5
Close On a Positive Note
"Either way, I'm very excited to join the team. Thank you for considering!" — protect the relationship, whatever the answer.
Pro Tip: Even if they agree verbally on the call — ask "Could you please share the revised details over email?" If it's not in email, it's not official.
💬 The WhatsApp Version

Half of Indian hiring happens on WhatsApp. If that's where HR is talking to you — use this short version.

WhatsApp Script
Hi [HR Name], thank you so much for the offer! I'm really excited about joining [Company]. 🙏

I had one small request — based on my skills and the current market range for this role, would it be possible to revisit the compensation to around ₹[X] LPA?

If we can work this out, I'm ready to accept right away. Thank you for considering!
WhatsApp Negotiation Rules
✅ Keep it short — 3 small paragraphs max
✅ One polite emoji is enough (🙏) — don't overdo it
✅ Give 24-48 hrs for a reply — don't spam
✅ Always confirm final numbers over email
❌ No voice notes — text stays professional
❌ No "Bro/Sir please" tone — confident and polite
Remember: WhatsApp is casual, but your tone shouldn't be. And after any agreement in chat — always ask: "Could you share the revised offer letter over email?"
🔒 When HR Says "Package Is Fixed"

The most common reply freshers hear. Most people give up here — but even when base can't move, you still have options.

1
Acknowledge — Don't Fight It
"I completely understand that the package is standardized for freshers."
Arguing with HR is a dead end. Accept it and move to step 2.
2
Pivot — Ask Beyond Base Salary
"In that case, would it be possible to consider a joining bonus, relocation assistance, or an early performance review at 6 months instead?"
Base may be fixed — these often aren't.
3
Get It In Writing
Whatever they agree to — "Could you please include this in the offer letter / confirm over email?" Verbal promises are forgotten after you join.
⚠️ 5 Mistakes That Kill Negotiations
MISTAKE #1
Faking a Competing Offer
"Company X is offering me more" — if HR asks for proof, you're trapped. Only mention offers you actually have.
MISTAKE #2
Giving Ultimatums
"Increase it or I won't join" — that's a threat, not a negotiation. Show flexibility, not demands.
MISTAKE #3
The "I Deserve" Tone
Entitlement kills goodwill instantly. Ask with a reason: "based on my skills and the market range."
MISTAKE #4
Negotiating After Accepting
Asking for more after signing looks unprofessional. Your window: after the offer, before you accept.
MISTAKE #5
Comparing With Friends' Packages
"My friend got 6 LPA" is irrelevant to HR. Cite market data instead — Glassdoor, AmbitionBox, LinkedIn.
💡 Bonus Tips — The Cheat Sheet

Quick rules to remember. 90% of negotiation success is in these basics.

💰 How much to ask? 10-20% above the offered CTC — a defensible range
⏰ When? After the offer letter, before accepting — the only window
📊 Research: Check role + city salaries on AmbitionBox, Glassdoor, LinkedIn
🙏 First response: Always thank them for any offer — negotiate after
📧 Always email: Verbal or chat agreements need email confirmation
😊 If it's a "no": Accept gracefully — then prove your worth in the first 6 months
