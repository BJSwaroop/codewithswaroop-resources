The only rule that matters

90 days, one path, in public

  • Days 1 to 30: learn the basics from the roadmap. Nothing else.
  • Days 31 to 60: build two small projects. Ugly is fine, finished is the point.
  • Days 61 to 90: post what you built, fix your resume and LinkedIn, start applying.

A roadmap you finish beats a roadmap that looks better on paper.
